# **App Name**: Noaah Tech Landing

## Core Features:

- Hero Section: Hero section with a bold headline and supporting subheadline.
- Feature Blocks: Feature blocks (3-4) with icons and short descriptions, similar to Fenesta's style.
- Product Showcase: Carousel to showcase products/services using icons or abstract visuals.
- Inquiry Form: Inquiry form with Name, Email, Phone, and Message fields.
- Contact Details: Contact details with address, phone number, and email placeholders.
- Footer: Footer with company name, copyright, and links to privacy policy and terms.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A dark blue (#1E3A8A) reflecting the darker shade in the logo.
- Secondary color: A lighter blue (#3B82F6) complementing the primary and reflecting the logo's lighter shade.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F2F5) providing a clean, uncluttered feel, similar to the logo's background.
- Accent color: White for call-to-actions to provide high contrast on the dark blue.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a modern and easy-to-read design.
- Minimalistic, uncluttered layout with ample whitespace and consistent design elements.
- Use modern and simple icons that visually represent the feature blocks.
- Subtle animations, such as fading transitions, for enhanced user experience.
